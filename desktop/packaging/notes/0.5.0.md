- Fermix keeps lists for you, a list per project, a shopping list or anything to tick off: ask in the chat, and the list shows under the reply.
- The Lists pane beside the chat, opened from a list's card or with Ctrl+Shift+L, is where you tick, add, reword, move and remove items, and bring back removed ones from Recently removed.
- The Cues pane shows your sources, watchers and mandates, and a task waiting for you shows in the tray.
- A watcher can set off a follow-up of your own, which asks first on a card in the chat with Run, Skip and Always allow, and Ask first again on the watcher's page takes Always allow back.
- A cue source can watch X accounts with the X sign-in you already have, and watchers can check items by meaning, not only by their keywords.
- On x86-64 computers, computer use reaches an app's controls on any desktop, X11 or Wayland, and the Computer pane says what it can do in this session.
- A task's proposals are cards in the chat, answered in place, and a time in the chat says its day when it is not today.
- Slack works on a computer with no public address.
